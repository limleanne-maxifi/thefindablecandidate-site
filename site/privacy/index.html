What I collect

Only what the product needs to work.

  • Unlock Your Findability (free): your email address, your score and band, which items you ticked, and which link brought you here. Collected when you submit the form — never before.
  • A Findability Check order: everything above, plus your full name, your public LinkedIn URL, the job title, market and seniority you're targeting, the skills you list, and the profile settings you report. You supply all of it yourself in the intake form.
  • Payment: handled entirely by Stripe. I never see or store your card details — I receive confirmation that you paid, the amount, and the email you used.
This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads nothing from third-party servers, and stores nothing in your browser. That's why there's no cookie banner — there's nothing to consent to.
What I do with it — and never do

Your data runs your Check. Nothing else.

I use what you give me to score your checklist, run the search you paid for, write your report, and email you the things you signed up for. Lawful basis, in the legal terms: your consent (checklist and emails) and performance of a contract (the Check you bought).

We will never sell, rent or share your data with anyone for their own purposes. Searches are run manually in a paid LinkedIn Recruiter seat, at your request, with your consent — no scraping, no bots, no extraction tools. Published analyses are anonymised to patterns only: no names, no screenshots of your profile, nothing identifying, unless you've given prior written permission.

How long I keep it

Fixed periods, then deletion.

  • Checklist leads: until you unsubscribe, or after 24 months without any interaction — whichever comes first.
  • Findability Check customers: order and delivery records kept 5 years, to meet Singapore accounting and tax record requirements.
  • Evidence packs (the screen recording and screenshots of your search): deleted 90 days after your Check is delivered. The window exists so a disputed finding can be re-verified; after it closes, the recording is gone.
Your rights

Access, correction, deletion — one email.

Email account@thefindablecandidate.com to see what I hold on you, correct it, or have it deleted. You'll get a substantive reply within 10 business days. Deletion removes you from the email list, the records sheets, and the evidence archive; the only thing retained is what Singapore law requires me to keep (payment records, for the accounting period).

If you're writing from the EU, UK or California: the same rights apply to you — access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection — through the same email, on the same timeline. This notice is written to PDPA (Singapore), and honours GDPR and CCPA requests equivalently.
